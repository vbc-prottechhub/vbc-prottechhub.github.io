Vienna BioCenter · Core Technology Hub

Decoding the language of proteins,
one molecule at a time.

The VBC Proteomics Technology Hub develops and applies next-generation technologies to measure, map, and understand the proteome. We partner with research groups across the campus to turn complex biological questions into quantitative, reproducible answers.
